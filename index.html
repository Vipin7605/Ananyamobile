A LITTLE HELP BEFORE YOU VISIT

Good questions.

Can I check a phone's price before visiting?

Absolutely. Use our phone finder or WhatsApp us with the model, storage and colour you want. We'll confirm current pricing and availability.

How do I get a repair estimate?

Select Mobile repair in the enquiry form and share your phone model and the issue. A final estimate and turnaround time may require an in-store inspection.

Do you offer EMI or exchange?

Ask us about options for your chosen device. Availability, eligibility, charges and exchange value need to be confirmed with the shop before purchase.

Can I place an order on this website?

This website helps you explore and enquire. Purchases and service arrangements are confirmed directly with the shop; sending an enquiry does not reserve a product or book a repair.
